# Guava Danish

<by: Sadh /><date: July 18, 2026 />

Last night I slept at 5am.  
I had company.  
So many of them.

I woke up today, and had coffee  
with a buddy  
and a guava danish  
while playback cafe kept playing  
tunes from the sea of Ithaca.

And then I saw Kylian and Saka  
wrestle for the bronze glory  
until Jude claims it all for himself.

And then I stared at the sun-kissed photos  
I took this week.  
And then I had a poke bowl for dinner.

And then I spent time  
doing nothing, noping and napping.  
Then I faced the everlasting  
and wrote this poem.
