# A Meeting

<by: Sadh /><date: July 14, 2026 />

they came
they sat
they rested

I observed
& took a long breath,
filled my lungs with fresh air
as the birds filled my gaze
and their own souls

they left
and I left

it was a meeting
lasted a minute
and an eternity
