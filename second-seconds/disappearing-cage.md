# The Disappearing Cage

<by: Sadh /><date: Jul 10, 2025 />

Fourteen years ago, I wrote about a cage  
I wrote a poem.  
A cage that held love, with love,  
An open cage, with door unlocked  
And a bird tucked in:  
Happy, free, and tethered wirelessly.

Fourteen years later,  
The cage disappeared  
And the bird remains tethered aimlessly.  
Love appeared, and disappeared.

I remained, at the same spot.  
Meanwhile, built a cage to nestle in  
And then burnt it to the ground  
To escape it.

Still.  
Still, I keep floating  
On the same hazy spot  
Between the mist and mystery:  
Borderlands of desire, despair, and joy.
